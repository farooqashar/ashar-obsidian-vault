## About Us

DeepInfer is managed by data scientists and machine learning researchers at [Surgical Planning Laboratory](http://www.spl.harvard.edu/) at the Harvard Medical School, Brigham & Women's Hospital and [Robotics and Control Laboratory](http://rcl.ece.ubc.ca/) at University of British Columbia.
