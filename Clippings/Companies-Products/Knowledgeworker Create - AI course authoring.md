## Knowledgeworker Create Authoring Tool FAQ

#### Is the authoring tool compatible with learning management systems and our existing IT environment?

### Compatibility of the Knowledgeworker Create Authoring Tool

Knowledgeworker Create integrates seamlessly with your learning management system and supports all modern e-learning standards, including xAPI (Tin Can), SCORM 2004, and SCORM 1.2—so your content can be easily deployed within existing platforms. Single sign-on (SSO) via SAML and optional APIs for custom integrations are also available.

#### Do I need any prior experience or technical skills to use the authoring tool?

### A drag-and-drop editor and a wide range of templates make it easy to get started

A wide range of templates, pre-built interaction elements, and ready-to-use question sets within the authoring tool help you create engaging learning content—automatically aligned with your corporate design. With an intuitive, template-based drag-and-drop editor, you save valuable time and resources—no coding required.

#### How well do the created courses perform across different devices, including tablets and smartphones?

### Fully responsive

With Knowledgeworker Create, you can create fully responsive online courses right from the start. The intuitive course editor lets you preview how your content will appear on different devices as you build—so you can make targeted adjustments and see your changes instantly.
