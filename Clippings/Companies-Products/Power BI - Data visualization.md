## Frequently asked questions

- **01/**
	### What is Power BI and what’s it used for?
	Power BI is a unified, scalable platform for self-service and enterprise business intelligence (BI). You can use it to connect to and visualize any data, and infuse the visuals into the apps you use every day.
- **02/**
	### What is a business intelligence platform?
	A business intelligence platform is a tool that connects, analyzes, and visualizes data so organizations can make better decisions. With Power BI, this means turning your data into interactive dashboards and insights that help teams understand performance and take action faster.
- **03/**
	### How should I choose a Power BI license for my organization?
	Power BI licenses aren’t exclusive—there are several ways to mix and match plans and licenses. For example, you could purchase Premium (P-SKU) or Microsoft Fabric (F-SKU) capacity to host the most popular reports, and then buy Power BI Pro licenses (or get them through Microsoft 365 E5) for your users who need to create and publish reports. Contact a sales expert to learn more.  
	  
	[Contact sales](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?linkid=2250307)
- **04/**
	### What is Power BI Embedded?
	Microsoft Power BI Embedded is a pay-as-you-go platform as a service (PaaS) offering. It allows app developers to embed fully interactive reports into their apps without having to build their own data visuals and controls. Power BI Embedded costs as little as $1 per hour for analytics, with no requirement for end-user licensing.  
	  
	[Try Power BI Embedded](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/p/?linkid=2216870)
- **05/**
	### What’s the difference between Microsoft Fabric and Power BI?
	Microsoft Fabric is an end-to-end analytics solution with full-service capabilities including data movement, data lakes, data engineering, data integration, data science, real-time analytics, data monitoring, and business intelligence—all backed by a shared platform providing robust data security, governance, and compliance.  
	  
	Power BI provides the business intelligence layer in  [Microsoft Fabric](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?linkid=2332534)  that enables you to gain insights from your data. Power BI Premium customers can simply turn on the Fabric tenant setting and access the rest of the product in a unified experience. And with its unified capacity model, Power BI Premium per capacity can be used against any Microsoft Fabric workload.
- **06/**
	### Can I access Microsoft Fabric if I'm a Power BI Pro customer?
	Power BI Pro customers can access Fabric workloads only through the Microsoft Fabric trial experience.  
	  
	[Try Microsoft Fabric](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-fabric/getting-started)
- **07/**
	### What features are available in Fabric SKUs compared to Power BI SKUs?
	Feature availability depends on both SKU and capacity level. See the [features list](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?linkid=2286423) for specific details.
- 08/
	### Are there free Power BI training and certifications available for my team?
	Yes, Microsoft offers free, self-paced Power BI training through Microsoft Learn, with structured learning paths that cover everything from getting started to building reports and modeling data. You can also take practice assessments to prepare for official certifications once your team is ready.  
	  
	[Explore learning paths](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?linkid=2280887)
- 09/
	### What are some Power BI dashboard examples?
	Microsoft provides a wide range of Power BI dashboard examples to help you get started. On Microsoft Learn, you can explore and download sample reports across scenarios like sales, customer profitability, IT spend, HR, and more, giving you a clear view of how dashboards are built and used.  
	  
	[Browse Power BI dashboard examples](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?linkid=2369711)
