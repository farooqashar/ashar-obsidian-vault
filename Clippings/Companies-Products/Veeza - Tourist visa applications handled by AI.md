## Frequently Asked Questions

Can I review my application before submission?

Yes. We share the prepared application with you so you can check your details and request corrections before submission.

What does Veeza actually do?

We prepare your visa application from your passport and the details you provide. A visa expert reviews your file, and full-service packages include appointment booking. Each destination card shows what is included.

Who prepares my application?

Veeza prepares your forms and documents from your passport and the details you provide. A visa expert checks the file before submission.

Which countries do you support?

You can explore Schengen, UK, US, and other available visa services in the country cards above. Each card shows the current package, price, and what is included. If your destination is not listed, chat with our team on WhatsApp.

What is included in the price?

Every package includes application preparation and expert review. Full-service packages also include appointment booking. The destination card shows any visa-centre fees included in your total. Separately payable government fees are identified before payment; UK and US government visa fees are paid directly to the government.

How and when do I pay?

It is free to start. Pay by card when your application is ready. Checkout shows the available payment options and total before you pay, and you receive a receipt.

Can I apply as a family or group?

Yes. You can apply with up to 5 travelers on the website. Use the plus and minus buttons above the country cards to see your group total. Any available group discount is included in the displayed price.

How long does it take?

Preparation time depends on your destination, the information you provide, and any documents still needed. Appointment availability and embassy processing times vary. We keep you updated as your application progresses.

What if appointments are not available?

For full-service packages, we continue looking and keep you updated. Availability and timing depend on the destination, so we cannot promise a particular date. With paperwork-only packages, you book the appointment yourself.

What if something goes wrong?

We keep you updated at every step via WhatsApp. If an issue comes up, our team steps in to resolve it. You can also reach us at info@veeza.ai.

Does Veeza guarantee my visa will be approved?

No. Visa decisions are made by the consulate or embassy of the destination country, not by Veeza. We help you submit an accurate, complete, and on-time application, which is what consulates look for. We do not offer legal visa advice.

What if my visa is rejected? Can I get a refund?

The embassy decides whether to approve your visa. Rejection does not entitle you to a service-fee refund. If an error on our side prevents us from completing the agreed service, we work to resolve it and may offer a partial or full refund under our Refund Policy. Official fees follow the relevant authority’s policy.

Is my passport data safe?

Yes. Your documents are encrypted in transit and at rest, which means they stay locked on the way to us and while we keep them. Only our qualified team can open them, and only if they need to check our AI's work. Bank statements, visa photos, travel insurance and business papers are optional to upload, and we'll ask if your application ever needs one. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Do you take my passport?

No. You upload a photo or scan and keep your physical passport. You provide it to the official visa centre or embassy when your application requires it.
