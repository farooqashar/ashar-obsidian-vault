No. WatchGuard delivers enterprise-grade security to businesses exclusively through a global network of trusted managed service provider and reseller partners.

MSPs use WatchGuard Cloud's multi-tenant console to deliver and manage security services for hundreds of customers at once. Businesses receive that same protection, configured and managed for them by a local WatchGuard partner.

WatchGuard Cloud is the interface that manages network, endpoint, identity, and cloud protection from one console, with AI-driven detection and response built in.

Rai is WatchGuard's built-in artificial intelligence layer that automates threat detection, investigation, and response across the platform, multiplying analyst capacity without added headcount.
