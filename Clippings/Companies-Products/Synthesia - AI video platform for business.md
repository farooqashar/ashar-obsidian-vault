## You’ve likely got a few questions

Yes, our Basic plan is free, with no credit card required. You can choose from our stock avatars, give them any outfit or scene you like, or design your own with Avatar Builder. Then you can generate videos in 140+ languages. You get 500 credits a month, enough for up to 10 minutes of video or 10 minutes of AI Dubbing. ### What can I do with Synthesia?

Synthesia is an AI video platform for business. You can create and manage professional video at scale, have real two-way conversations with realistic Interactive Avatars, and build our AI video technology into your own product.

**Video:**

- **Create:** Describe what you need to Assistant, or start from a PowerPoint, document, or link, and get a finished draft with a script, scenes, and an avatar presenter. Choose from hundreds of [AI avatars](https://www.synthesia.io/features/avatars), design your own with Avatar Builder, or present as yourself with a Personal Avatar.
- **Translate:** Make videos in 140+ languages, translate finished videos with 1-Click Translation, and dub any existing video with [AI Dubbing](https://www.synthesia.io/features/ai-dubbing) in the original speaker's voice.
- **Manage:** Keep every video on brand with Brand Kits, edit together in real time with Live Collaboration, and organize content across teams in shared workspaces.
- **Publish:** Share by link, embed anywhere, or export to your LMS with SCORM. Videos update automatically when you edit them, and Analytics shows you who's watching and where they drop off.
- **Engage:** Add quizzes, clickable CTAs, and branching paths so viewers do more than watch.

**Sessions:**

- **Roleplay Sessions:** Practice hard conversations, like cold calls or tough feedback, with an avatar that responds and pushes back, then get coached and scored in real time.
- **Survey Sessions:** Get the depth of an interview with the reach of a form. An avatar asks your questions and follows up on what people actually say. You get back themes and sentiment across every response, plus full transcripts.

**Build:**

- **API:** Generate videos programmatically with the Synthesia API, or add real-time [Interactive Avatars](https://www.synthesia.io/features/avatars/interactive-avatars) to your own product, website, or support flow with the Interactive Avatars API.

Teams use Synthesia to [create training videos](https://www.synthesia.io/learning-and-development), onboard new hires, enable sales teams, share internal updates, gather customer insight, and support customers.

### Can I create an AI avatar of myself?

Yes. Upload a single photo or a short video, and we'll build a Personal Avatar that looks like you. You can put it in any outfit or space and have it present any script. You can also clone your voice to go with it. Every Personal Avatar needs a live, on-camera consent recording, so no one can make an avatar of you without your permission.

### Can I translate and dub my videos into other languages?

Yes. You can make any Synthesia video in 140+ languages and voices. Once a video is finished, 1-Click Translation turns it into other languages without rebuilding it. You can also [translate videos](https://www.synthesia.io/features/video-translator) made outside Synthesia by uploading a file or pasting a YouTube link. The dubbed version keeps the original speaker's voice and accent. Lip sync is optional, and a Custom Glossary keeps brand terms and product names consistent. Each dub keeps the speaker's own voice with matching lip sync.

### What are Sessions?

Sessions are real, back-and-forth conversations with a lifelike avatar, at any scale.

- **Roleplay Sessions:** Your team practices hard conversations, like cold calls or tough feedback, with an avatar that pushes back, then gets coached and scored in real time.
- **Survey Sessions:** An avatar asks your questions, follows up on what people say, and hands back themes and sentiment across every response.

### Can I customize AI videos to add brand identity?

Yes. You can add your logo, colors, and fonts, and upload your own images, footage, and voiceovers. Brand Kits apply your company's style to every video your team creates. You can dress avatars in branded outfits and place them in your own spaces, or create a brand mascot with Avatar Builder.

### Does Synthesia integrate with my LMS?

Yes. Synthesia is designed to work with your existing Learning Management System (LMS). You can embed videos directly into your courses and training modules, or export them as SCORM packages, including every translated version. Embedded videos update automatically when you edit them, so you don't have to re-upload them every time your training changes. [See the full list of integrations](https://docs.synthesia.io/docs/synthesia-integrations).

### Is it safe for businesses to create AI videos in Synthesia?

Yes. Synthesia is SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, ISO 27701, and ISO 42001 certified, and GDPR compliant, with EU data residency. Enterprise teams get SSO and SCIM for access control. Every video goes through content moderation, and no one's likeness becomes an avatar without their explicit consent. Read more about our [security](https://security.synthesia.io/) and [responsible AI](https://www.synthesia.io/responsible-ai) policies.
