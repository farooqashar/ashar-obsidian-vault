### Eco

Respect for the planet. Every answer uses energy — but not every question needs the most energy-hungry model. discode picks the right one and shows you what each answer costs: you and the planet.

[Learn more](https://discode.ai/eco)

[![Sven Heimdall — Data Bouncer](https://discode.ai/_next/image?url=%2Fillustrations%2FSven_Heimdall_Portrait2.png&w=3840&q=75&dpl=dpl_AMxX7JojmfUpAVY29LK7bvNoBZHw)](https://discode.ai/private)

### Private

Keep sensitive data where it belongs: on your device. Not in the cloud, not with an LLM overseas. A local assistant detects sensitive data and suggests anonymizing it before anything leaves. You decide what’s allowed through — only as much as the answer really needs.

[Learn more](https://discode.ai/private)

[![Veritas Jones](https://discode.ai/_next/image?url=%2Fillustrations%2FVeritas_Jones_Portrait2.png&w=3840&q=75&dpl=dpl_AMxX7JojmfUpAVY29LK7bvNoBZHw)](https://discode.ai/true)

### True

AI often sounds most convincing exactly when it’s flat-out wrong. When it really matters, discode helps you guard against hallucinations: switch on the multi-model check and several independent models cross-check each other. More resources — your call.

[Learn more](https://discode.ai/true)

### Smart

discode nerds out down the rabbit hole for you: every day we dig through the latest benchmarks, test new models and read ALL the reviews — so you don’t have to. Set what matters to you with the turntables, and discode picks the right model for every task.

[Learn more](https://discode.ai/smart)

### The discode Crew

Gloria Greenor, Sven Heimdall, Veritas Jones, and DJ Smarty McFly are a well-rehearsed team that keeps the disco running. Together they keep an eye on the CO₂ footprint of every request, guard your data like a bouncer, put answers to the test when it counts, and pick the right AI model for every task. Four characters, one goal — an AI that works for you, not the other way around.
