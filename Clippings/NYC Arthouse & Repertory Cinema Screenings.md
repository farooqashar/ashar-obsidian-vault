### What is repertory.nyc?

I built repertory.nyc in my free time as a way to aggregate films from the independent theaters I love. This site exists to highlight the vast and vibrant film community in New York City, while also making it more accessible to navigate. I am always looking to add more theaters and screenings to the site. If you have suggestions, please reach out to me at [jacklilleyerington@gmail.com](mailto:jacklilleyerington@gmail.com), or check me out at [jacklilleyerington.com](https://jacklilleyerington.com/).
