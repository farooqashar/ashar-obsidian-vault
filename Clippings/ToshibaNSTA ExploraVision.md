Kick Off ExploraVision

[Get Started with ExploraVision](https://zoom.us/clips/share/hx2DbDVkQqO-gYEN70_TfA)

Toshiba/NSTA ExploraVisionempowers young minds to imagine the future in 10 or more years and and create innovative solutions for the world’s challenges through our **[STEM competition](https://www.exploravision.org/description/)**. Our comprehensive [**STEM project format**](https://www.exploravision.org/project-format/) [**guides**](https://www.exploravision.org/project-format/) students through every step of the research and development process, fostering **critical thinking** and **scientific inquiry**. By participating in ExploraVision **STEM competition**, students not only enhance their STEM skills but also gain valuable experience in teamwork and problem-solving. Join ExploraVision to inspire future scientists and engineers and unlock a world of [**STEM education opportunities**.](https://www.exploravision.org/for-teachers/)
