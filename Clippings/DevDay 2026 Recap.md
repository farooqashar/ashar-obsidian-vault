DevDay 2026 is our biggest yet, with more than 20 major announcements across ChatGPT, Codex, our models, and entirely new forms of working with AI.

We believe AI can help bring about a new renaissance of creativity and discovery. It should give people more time for what matters to them, more freedom to pursue their ideas, and the ability to do things they didn’t think were possible.

Today, we introduced agents that can take on ongoing responsibilities and new ways for people and AI to work together. We also expanded our commitment to an open ecosystem by opening up ChatGPT as a shared surface where humans and agents can collaborate and where developers can directly launch new native experiences to our collective 1.2B weekly users.

Here’s everything we announced.

## More customization in ChatGPT with plugins

![Canva plugin extension shown in the ChatGPT sidebar.](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/3qtFeAk5RsusYh1lcZZvqQ/932764cabaff4e9fd5de4df43d26042e/PLUGIN_EXTENSIONS.png?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp) ![Figma, Shopify, and plugin cards on a blue background.](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/6rVkRsIQFtKsSRuC3CXs3Z/d63f06d6a28913afafe8095ef30b84c5/Improved_plugin_creation__submission__and_discovery.png?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp) ![Three example websites created with Sites.](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/1jKtNyWGnsW4vkoCNLpGa1/24c0650f734cbda75e25eaeb2fcde438/Sites_with_connected_plugins.png?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp) ![Diagram linking MCP Events with automations.](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/6EqU2x1zGv6aw9ylaq504B/923c699ea1f9e549c0440ec4bc349517/MCP_events_for_Plugin_automations.png?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp)

We’re opening the platform we use to build ChatGPT features so developers can create their own experiences within ChatGPT. Plugin extensions let you give your plugin a home in the sidebar and build interactive panels where people can work alongside the conversation. You can also create viewers for the file types your product supports.

Available to all plans. [Learn more ⁠](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/build/extensions)

Now you can bring colleagues together as a team in ChatGPT to share pages, slides, plugins, and spreadsheets. You can also delegate recurring work such as weekly project updates to team tasks. ChatGPT uses connected tools to gather information and take action on a schedule or in response to events like a new email or Slack message. Teammates can refine the instructions together as priorities change.

Available to all Business and Enterprise plans. [Learn more ⁠](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/team-tasks)

## Do more with your ChatGPT subscription

![Sign in modal for ChatGPT in Dactyl](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/Ol9N30uwCXshH4PttWTjP/e8d794715425dee799f160a316542b8e/SIGN_IN_WITH_CHATGPT.png?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp) ![Pro 500 with Ultrafast lettering on a starfield.](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/vLMMtGVk0E5bPSShgIGGc/c20e4f7d8ccd7be497ba594e50a983b8/PRO_500_.png?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp) ![OpenAI Marketplace title and storefront icon surrounded by partner logos.](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/7FWZCS8PGje6ckOCj5Ke6j/d6e9987b5a9af18d2027670100a018d7/OPENAI_MARKETPLACE.png?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp)

We’re making it easier to use your ChatGPT plan with other tools. Use your allowance across 16 partners including Cognition’s Devin, Notion, Vercel, T3, OpenClaw, and Dactyl, and control how much each can use. Eligible OpenAI usage counts toward your plan limits. You can also sign in to a range of tools with your ChatGPT account to connect plugins faster and manage fewer passwords.

Identity is available globally. Plan usage is available to Plus and Pro users in participating tools. [Learn more ⁠](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001542)
