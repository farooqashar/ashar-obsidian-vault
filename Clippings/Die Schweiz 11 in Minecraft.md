The small print for all of Switzerland
1. **Demanding.** The world is built while you play: wherever you go for the first time, the game builds the blocks from the world package. That takes a powerful computer with 16 GB of memory, and on slower ones it takes longer. All of Switzerland has been tested on a Windows PC (AMD Ryzen 7 7800X3D, NVIDIA RTX 4070, 32 GB) and a MacBook Pro (Apple M1 Max, 64 GB); similar hardware is recommended. The free Jungfrau-Aletsch area shows whether it runs on yours, with the same setup.
2. **Border.** 41,285 km² is the area of Switzerland. The world, however, fills the whole rectangle around Switzerland, including parts of the neighbouring countries. There, and in places right at the border, the terrain only comes from the Copernicus elevation model with one point every 30 m instead of from swisstopo: coarser and with fewer details. Beyond the border the world is patchy or flat in places, and where building data is missing, trees stand in place of houses, for example near Geneva.
3. **Not equally detailed everywhere.** How much detail a region has depends on the data that exists for it, so it varies from region to region.
4. **1:1 scale, not a 1:1 copy.** Every block is one metre, and mountains, lakes and valleys are exactly where they are. But the world is built by a program from open data, not by hand. Buildings are simplified, and in places bridges, roads, trees or waters are wrong or missing. The world has been checked by sampling, not metre by metre, so there can be patchy, flat or wrong spots anywhere. If you spot a mistake, write to me; it helps me improve later versions.

You pay for the licence to the world package, the world calculated from geodata. Mod, texture pack and shaders are free and come just the same with the free Jungfrau-Aletsch area.

## Ten minutes to the Alps.

Setup

### Prism Launcher

Download the free Prism Launcher from prismlauncher.org and sign in with your Microsoft account.

### Import the ZIP

In Prism, click `Add Instance → Import` and choose the downloaded ZIP. Prism fetches Forge and Java 8 by itself.

### Create the world

Start the instance, then `Singleplayer → Create New World → More World Options`, and click `World Type` until it shows `generator.schweiz`. You start in the middle of the area. The first visit to a place takes a few minutes.

### Shaders, optional

For the light in the videos: download [OptiFine 1.12.2 HD U F5](https://optifine.net/downloads) (section “Minecraft 1.12.2”, under “Show all versions”) and put it in the instance’s `mods` folder. The Manifold shaders are already selected.

## Questions

FAQ

Does it run on Bedrock, consoles or phones?

No, only on Minecraft Java Edition for Windows, macOS and Linux. The world needs the Cubic Chunks mod, which only exists for Java.

Why Minecraft 1.12.2 and not the latest version?

Vanilla Minecraft is at most 384 blocks tall. Switzerland ranges from 193 m at Lago Maggiore to 4,634 m on the Dufourspitze. Cubic Chunks lifts that limit, and it runs stably on 1.12.2.

Where does the data come from?

From the open geodata of swisstopo, the Swiss Federal Office of Topography: swissALTI3D for the terrain, swissTLM3D for water, forest, dams and avalanche barriers. The data has been free to use since 2021.

What does all of Switzerland cost?

15 CHF, or more if you like. Every later update is included: with your licence key you always get the newest version. Jungfrau-Aletsch stays free, without sign-up.

Is the world finished?

Not yet, it is in early access. All of Switzerland is playable, but not yet everywhere the way I want it: water and rock faces are to look nicer, gaps at the border and below Lago Maggiore are to be closed, and an updater is to install new versions by itself. You download every later version for free with your licence key. There is no date for any of this.

Can I return the world?

No, and there is no refund, not even if it runs poorly on your computer. So please try the free Jungfrau-Aletsch area first: it uses exactly the same setup as all of Switzerland. If you run into a problem, write to me anyway, and I will help where I can.

Can I buy from abroad?

No. For legal reasons I sell all of Switzerland only to people who live in Switzerland. At checkout you therefore enter an address in Switzerland and confirm that you live here. Where you happen to be when buying doesn’t matter. The free Jungfrau-Aletsch area can be downloaded from anywhere.

Can I play with friends?

Not yet. Your own Forge server running the same world should work, but it has not been tried. Every player would still need the package on their own computer, because the distant view is read locally.

The import in Prism fails with “Failed to extract file …”. What can I do?

Usually the ZIP was damaged during the download. On some connections a long download breaks off in the browser, often around 2 GB. Afterwards the file may even have the right size (24,169,484,840 bytes), but parts of it are broken. Download the ZIP again with a download manager such as the [Free Download Manager](https://www.freedownloadmanager.org/). It loads the file in pieces and gets around the problem. Before importing again, delete the folder `.tmp` in Prism under *Folders → Instances*: failed attempts are left there and take up space.
