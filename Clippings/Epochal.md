Simulate the past. Predict the future.

## IntroducingTime Portal

A point-in-time environment where AI agents search the internet as it existed on a past date.

- ### RL environments

	Turn the web into a controlled, reproducible environment for RL training.
- ### Forecasting

	Evaluate how well AI models predict the future.
- ### Backtesting

	Test strategies over historical data.
- ### Data generation

	Fill gaps in historical data using only what was known at the time.
- ### Security research

	Train auditors to find vulnerabilities using only advisories published before the fix.
- ### Post-mortems

	Replay what your team could have known before a call went wrong.
