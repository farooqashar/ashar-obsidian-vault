## From today's featured article

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/26/City_Hall_Station_%2832200%29.jpg/250px-City_Hall_Station_%2832200%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=api&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://edjefferson.com/localwiki/?w=City_Hall_station_%28IRT_Lexington_Avenue_Line%29)

The **[City Hall station](https://edjefferson.com/localwiki/?w=City_Hall_station_%28IRT_Lexington_Avenue_Line%29 "City Hall station (IRT Lexington Avenue Line)")**, also known as City Hall Loop station, is a closed station on the IRT Lexington Avenue Line of the New York City Subway. It is located under City Hall Park, next to New York City Hall, in the Civic Center neighborhood of Manhattan in New York City. The station was constructed for the Interborough Rapid Transit Company as the southern terminal of the city's first subway line, which was approved in 1900. Construction of the segment of the line that includes the City Hall station started on September 12 of the same year.
