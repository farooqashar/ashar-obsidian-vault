### How is Wingman different from ChatGPT or Claude?

They chat. Wingman works. ChatGPT and Claude are great for thinking, writing, and asking questions, but they aren't connected to your actual work tools. Wingman plugs into your inbox, calendar, CRM, social accounts, and 300+ other apps. It doesn't just suggest what to do. It sends the email, books the meeting, updates the CRM, and drafts the post. And it always asks before anything consequential, so nothing happens that you didn't approve.

### How much does Wingman cost?

You start for free. Your first few credits and use cases are on us. After that, your first month is $1 with 100 credits to try everything. Light tasks like a daily brief use 1-2 credits; deeper work like research uses 3-5. Cancel anytime. No hidden fees, no credit card to start.

### What apps and tools does Wingman connect to?

300+ integrations and growing: Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar, Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce, GitHub, LinkedIn, X, Notion, and more. You sign in once via OAuth or API keys. Wingman only asks for the permissions a real assistant would need, and you can revoke any connection in one tap.

### Is my data safe with Wingman?

Yes. Every credential Wingman touches is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS in transit. Nothing is stored in plain text. Every job runs in its own isolated sandbox. Wingman is SOC 2 Type I via Emergent, GDPR aligned, and was independently red-teamed by Straiker STAR Labs in April 2026. You can revoke all access or hit the global kill switch in one tap, anytime.
