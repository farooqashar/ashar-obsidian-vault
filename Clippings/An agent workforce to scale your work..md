## Frequently asked questions

Is this a model gateway or a router?

No. A gateway forwards your tokens to whichever model you name; a router load-balances. Grep runs the entire job. It picks the model for each step against your cost ceiling and accuracy floor, executes in isolated sandboxes, and writes the audit trail. The model choice is an output, not your input.

Do I have to talk to sales to try it?

No. Self-serve signup runs an agent the same day, no sales contact. Self-serve is a headline feature, not a consolation path. Enterprise teams spending $50K+/month get a working session. The product is never gated or waitlisted.

Which models do you use?

Claude, GPT, DeepSeek, and secure open-source models, chosen per step. Cheap, fast models handle ingestion and extraction; frontier models are reserved for the steps where accuracy is non-negotiable. You set the constraints; the harness routes.

How is “managed” different from a provider’s managed offering?

A provider optimizes for its own models. It wins when you spend more on them. Grep is not a model. We optimize across all of them for your constraints, and we win when you win. Managed is not Claude-managed.

What do you give compliance and risk teams?

A decision trail on every run: the model chosen, the cost, and the reason for each step, exportable as a file you can hand to a regulator. Sandbox isolation, scoped keys, and SOC 2 Type II compliance are standard.
