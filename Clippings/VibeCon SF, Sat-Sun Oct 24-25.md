How We Are Doing This

If you build something extraordinary and want to prove it, this is your room.

VibeCon is a builder competition for the top 200 builders in the USA. But here's the thing: you don't just sign up. You have to earn your spot by proving you actually belong there. Once selected, you compete in the VibeCon Battle Grid, a two-day event with the other 199 builders.

Most events work the same way: you fill out a form and hope for the best. VibeCon is the opposite. You get selected because you've already shown you can build at a high level, not because you submitted an application. We're filtering for actual signal, not just the number of people who apply.
