## Questions? We've Got Answers

What is ThankView and how does it support stewardship?

ThankView by EverTrue is a video messaging platform designed to help fundraising teams create personal, meaningful outreach at scale. It makes it easy to send thank-yous, updates, invitations, and impact stories in a format donors actually engage with.

Do donors need to download anything to watch a video?

No. ThankView’s fundraising videos open instantly in any browser—on mobile or desktop—without logins or apps.

Can we personalize videos at scale?

Yes. ThankView supports personalized names, captions, landing pages, and segmented audiences so every video message feels intentional.

How does ThankView help measure donor engagement?

You can track opens, views, watch time, and actions (like clicking a donation link) to see which video messages resonate with supporters and who’s engaging most.

Is ThankView easy for non-technical team members to use?

Absolutely. Anyone on your team can record a video, select a template, and send it in minutes. No editing experience required.

Can we automate stewardship campaigns?

Yes. Build automated video sequences for everything from giving day follow-up to event recaps to scholarship messages.

Does ThankView integrate with our CRM?

There’s a good chance! ThankView connects with leading CRM and giving platforms, so your donor data and engagement insights stay up to date.
