GEICO strongly encourages taking defensive driving courses, and in turn, you could qualify for a discount that lets you enjoy a [cheaper car insurance](https://www.geico.com/auto-insurance/cheap-auto-insurance/) policy.

## What are defensive driving courses?

Defensive driving courses teach safe driving techniques for car accident prevention. Essentially, you will learn how to drive respectfully in relation to other motorists.

Adopting defensive driver techniques, like maintaining a safe distance between cars or limiting the force of impact, could help make the roads a safer place. That's why GEICO may be able to offer you a discount for taking a course to learn these skills.

Eligibility rules and requirements for defensive driving discounts vary by state.

We're so glad you've decided to take the next step in improving your driving skills. Log in to check your discount status or select a state to find defensive driving discounts and courses.

## New York Accident Prevention Information & Courses

New York drivers could save up to 10% on applicable coverages by completing an approved accident prevention course.

GEICO policyholders who wish to take an online course through Defensive Driving by IMPROV, American Safety Council, the National Safety Council, the New York Safety Program or Driver Training Associates, Inc. should follow these links:

- [Defensive Driving by IMPROV](https://www.myimprov.com/geico/new-york/)
- [American Safety Council](https://www.safemotorist.com/geico-defensive-driving/new-york/)
- [National Safety Council](https://www.drivesafe.com/geicony/)
- [New York Safety Program](https://www.thesafetyprogram.com/GEICO/index.php?a_aid=4ffb37b4dc694&a_bid=da44b78f)
- [Driver Training Associates, Inc.](https://ticketschool.com/courses/geico-ny)

Completing one of these online courses offers eligible policyholders the following benefits:

- **Convenient processing**; after successful completion, GEICO will be notified and your policy will be updated to reflect the discount
- **Discounted price** for GEICO policyholders

New York's DMV website has a list of either available in-person or [classroom accident prevention courses](https://dmv.ny.gov/pirp/classroom) as well as [online and alternative accident prevention courses](https://dmv.ny.gov/pirp/online). Please review this list to find the most convenient course provider for you.

In order to receive credit from GEICO for other courses, eligible policyholders can easily submit a copy of the course completion certificate online.

1. Simply [log in to your policy](https://ecams.geico.com/login?start=PG43) once your course is complete
2. Follow the instructions on the Discounts page

Once the certificate has been processed, we will notify you of the savings.
