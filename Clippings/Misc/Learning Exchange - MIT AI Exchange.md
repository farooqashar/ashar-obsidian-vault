## Learning Exchange

Education in the AI era: teaching with AI, teaching about AI, and understanding how AI changes learning.

The MIT AI Learning Exchange works with the Teaching and Learning Lab to support instructors and students through communities of practice, course adaptation support, policy guidance and AI literacy.

## What we do

- ### Communities of practice
	Seminars, tutorials, AI lunches and workshops where instructors share approaches to teaching with AI and learn from colleagues in other fields.
	1 upcoming event
- ### Course adaptation
	Teaching assistants work with faculty to build AI-aware assignments and learning tools, revising course objectives, assessments and reusable teaching materials.
	1 upcoming event
- ### Policies and guidance
	Templates and examples that help instructors set clear expectations for AI use in assignments, exams, projects and other academic work.
	1 upcoming event
- ### AI literacy
	Teaching resources that help students understand AI tools, their limits, and responsible use in their fields.
	1 upcoming event
- ### Residential learning and community
	Activities that support face-to-face learning and connection among students, including learning together away from screens.
	2 upcoming events
