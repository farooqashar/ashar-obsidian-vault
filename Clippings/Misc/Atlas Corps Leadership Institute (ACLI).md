## Leadership potential knows no borders.

**Atlas Corps Leadership Institute (ACLI)** is designed for globally minded early‑career professionals (typically aged 21–32) seeking to build leadership resilience, a global community, and career‑ready skills without geographic or visa barriers. It is an accessible, virtual-first program that offers transformational leadership skill development and human-centered capabilities sought by employers worldwide.

**As part of our commitment to the Cultural Vistas alumni community, eligible alumni receive a reduced participation fee for ACLI.**

By joining ACLI, you become a member of an international community & network of belonging to develop self-efficacy & resilience, interpersonal effectiveness, and cross-cultural adaptability.

Over 12 weeks, you’ll participate in live sessions with invited guest speakers, enhance your leadership skills through self-paced assignments, collaborate with peers across cultures, and earn a digital credential to demonstrate your growth.

Launched in 2020 as the Virtual Leadership Institute (VLI), the program has helped 570+ young professionals from over 80 countries develop resilience, adaptability & collaboration skills essential in today’s rapidly changing world.

Today, the ACLI continues to provide a unique platform for leaders from around the globe to engage in professional development while fostering a network of collaboration and mutual support.

## Program Details

#### The Atlas Corps Leadership Institute invites participants to:

- Build connections within a global network of emerging leaders, fostering collaboration and lifelong professional relationships.
- Develop critical leadership skills through immersive virtual workshops, self-guided learning, and interactive small group engagement with peers.
- Learn from leading U.S. organizations and experts on topics such as self-management and resilience, conflict resolution, and intercultural adaptability.
- Explore strategies to address pressing global challenges and drive meaningful impact.
- Gain insights into how global and local leaders work together to create sustainable solutions in diverse team environments.
- Experience the transformative power of peer mentoring to refine personal and professional goals and align with a leadership style that bolsters your context and goals.

## Eligibility & Application Process

Step 1

### Check Eligibility

Applicants must meet the program’s professional experience and educational requirements:

- Bachelor’s degree with 1–3 years of experience OR a high school degree/GED with 3–5 years of experience.
- Proficiency in English (speaking, reading, and writing).
- Availability to dedicate 3–5 hours per week to programming activities for 12 weeks.
- Technological capacity to participate in virtual sessions (desktop or laptop preferred).

We welcome applicants from diverse backgrounds who are curious, motivated to learn, and interested in building global connections.

Step 2

### Submit an Application

Eligible candidates must complete an online application form. Candidates are required to provide background information, a record of their professional experience, and respond to short essay and video questions. The application form should take 15-20 minutes to complete.

**Recruitment for ACLI is currently closed.** [Click here](https://culturalvistas.co1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_5myMCbxDQ7xJ2ya) to join the waitlist and be notified when the next cycle opens.

For additional questions or support about the application process, email [institute@atlascorps.org](mailto:institute@atlascorps.org).

Step 3

### Application Evaluation

Applications will be reviewed by a selection committee to assess eligibility, experience, and alignment with the program’s goals. Applications will be reviewed and accepted on a rolling basis. All applicants will be notified by email if they are selected to proceed.

Step 4

### Final Selection & Payment

Selected participants will be notified via email and will be required to sign a participation agreement and complete the payment of program fees.

**Important:** If selected, your place will only be confirmed after payment has been made or a scholarship agreement has been signed.

Step 5

### Pre-Orientation Assignments

Accepted participants will complete pre-orientation activities and assignments in preparation for the program’s start.

## Our Partners & Supporters

Since 2021, Atlas Corps Leadership Institute has partnered with many public and non-governmental organizations to offer a variety of opportunities for our Scholars. The ACLI has been made possible through generous support of several U.S. Embassies and private foundations. Through these partnerships, Atlas Corps has hosted international and one-country cohorts with specific themes, such as strengthening climate resilience, empowering women, or fostering economic development. Our partners have supported the Atlas Corps Virtual Leadership Institute in our efforts to recruit suitable program participants and impact our programming by providing valuable resources and support directly to the Scholars.
