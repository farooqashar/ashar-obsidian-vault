Microsoft is [extending a shared foundational harness](https://aka.ms/copilot-harness/announce) across Copilot experiences, bringing powerful advances in agentic reasoning, tool use, and agent orchestration to products across Microsoft. [Microsoft Copilot Studio](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/microsoft-copilot-studio/) makers are already building with this technology through what was previously called the GitHub Copilot harness. Now, to reflect its broader role across Microsoft, we’re updating the name to the **Microsoft Copilot harness**.

For Copilot Studio makers, while the harness name is changing, the innovation behind it isn’t slowing down. The Microsoft Copilot harness is already powering some of our most exciting advances in agent capabilities, combining sophisticated reasoning, tool use, and orchestration to help makers build agents designed to tackle complex challenges and take meaningful action.

Your existing agents will continue working as they do today, with no changes to capabilities or billing. Over the coming weeks, you’ll begin seeing the updated terminology throughout the product.

These findings demonstrate how a harness can impact agent performance. By helping agents use reasoning models more effectively, recover from failures, and make better use of model resources, the Microsoft Copilot harness is helping organizations unlock more value from the models they already use.

And we’re building on that foundation to help makers tackle even bigger challenges. Recently, we announced new capabilities for , including agent accounts, business process memory, and triggers in the Microsoft Copilot harness.

Together, these capabilities open up new possibilities for delegating ongoing business responsibilities to agents, while keeping people in control of important decisions. For Copilot Studio makers, it’s another way the Microsoft Copilot harness can help turn ambitious ideas into practical business solutions.

## Real results, real-world impact

The possibilities are exciting, but what’s already happening in practice is just as compelling. At Microsoft, we’re seeing firsthand what agents powered by the Microsoft Copilot harness can accomplish. Microsoft Customer Service and Support Business Applications built a dynamic resolution agent that uses reasoning to analyze support cases, draws on existing knowledge and tools. In August 2026, the agent achieved a fully autonomous resolution rate—defined as resolving cases without human intervention—of over 35%.
