## 1 in 6 kids in the U.S.

## — Almost 12 million —

## are growing up in poverty.

That's a lot of kids — kids who aren't getting what they need to grow up healthy and strong. Kids in cities, suburbs, and small towns, in nearly every neighborhood across the country.

This can feel too big to fix. Or like it's just the way things are. But it's not — the solutions exist, and if we each pitch in, we can solve it.

Here’s how we start.
