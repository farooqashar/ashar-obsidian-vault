Looking to earn your associate degree? The CUNY and SUNY Reconnect programs can help.

## Overview

The [SUNY](https://www.suny.edu/communitycollege/free-cc/) and [CUNY](https://www.cuny.edu/admissions/reconnect/) Reconnect initiative offers free tuition, books, supplies, and waived fees at community colleges for New York residents ages 25–55 without a college degree who enroll in eligible high-demand associate or bachelor's degree programs.

The program connects eligible New Yorkers to many job opportunities in high-demand fields including, but not limited to:

- Advanced manufacturing
- Artificial Intelligence
- Cybersecurity
- Engineering
- Technology
- Nursing and allied health fields
- Green and renewable energy
- Pathways to teaching in shortage areas
- Supply chain and logistics
- Air traffic control and aviation management
- Emergency management

---

## Eligibility

- New York State resident or eligible for in-state tuition.
- Aged 25–55 as of January 1 of the semester they apply.
- No prior postsecondary degree.
- Planning to study full-time or part-time in an eligible degree program; enroll in a minimum of 6 credits per semester and 12 per academic year.
- File [FAFSA](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa) and apply for [NYS TAP](https://www.ny.gov/services/apply-new-york-state-tap) or the [NYS DREAM Act](https://hesc.ny.gov/applying-aid/nys-dream-act), if eligible.
