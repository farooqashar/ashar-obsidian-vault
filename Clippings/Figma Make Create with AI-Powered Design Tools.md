## Figma Make features

- ### Make on your local codebase (Beta)
	New
	Convert your Figma designs into localized, production-ready assets in Make's visual software editor. Coming soon.
- ### Custom skills
	New
	Package your most frequent workflows into a reusable set of instructions for Make.
- ### MCP connectors
	Connect to external tools via the MCP, allowing you to work with documents, tasks, and data from other apps as context for your app or prototype.
- ### Make kits
	Sync your npm packages, library styles, and guidelines directly with Figma Make to generate high-fidelity prototypes.
- ### Plan mode
	New
	Plan before building. Clarify intent and create a structured, editable plan before generation starts.
- ### Attachments
	Attach designs, images, videos, audio, text files, and PDFs to your prompts to guide the AI toward better results.
- ### Comments
	Give and receive feedback on a prototype or web app built in Figma Make.
- ### Annotations
	Select elements, prompt, and send all changes to the model at once.
- ### Version history
	Track every AI and manual edit. Allows you to preview, favorite, or restore any iteration in your project.
- ### Voice-to-text prompting
	Dictate your ideas to Figma Make to generate your protoype.
- ### Make mobile
	Open a Make on your phone, experience it natively, and view and respond to comments directly in context.
- ### Point and edit
	Refine your prototypes by clicking elements to style, swap, or prompt for changes directly in the preview.
- ### Templates
	Save and reuse proven workflows, turning one Make into a repeatable way of working.
- ### Custom fonts
	Add custom fonts from your computer or shared with your organization.
