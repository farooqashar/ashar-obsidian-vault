The unusually helpful AI work assistant.

[Meet your Townie](https://www.town.com/sign-up)

![Bud the dog Townie wearing a bowtie](https://www.town.com/_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fbrand%2Fhome%2Ftownies%2Fbud.webp&w=640&q=90&dpl=dpl_bP1RENDtQ4AdUSWtdFsV1mwmjDQN)

Your work is spread across inboxes, calendars, chats, and apps.

Your Townie works in all of them, learning your people, projects, and voice, and gets the work done with you.

Nothing you connect trains a model, by default. SOC2 Type 2 compliant.

### SOC II Type 2 compliant

Town's security controls are independently audited and meet enterprise-grade standards.

### Your Townie asks first

By default, your Townie asks before anything important goes out. You decide what they can do on their own.

### No model training

By default, your data is never used to train AI models, by Town or by our providers.

### Encrypted in transit and at rest

Your data is encrypted everywhere it goes and everywhere it lives. US-hosted on Convex and AWS.
