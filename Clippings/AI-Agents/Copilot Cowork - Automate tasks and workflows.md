## Frequently asked questions

- 01/
	### What is Cowork?
	Copilot Cowork completes multi-step tasks on your behalf. You describe the outcome, and it grounds the work in your emails, meetings, files, and business data powered by Work IQ. Cowork plans the steps, picks the right skills and apps, pauses for your approval at key checkpoints, and returns finished artifacts—a deck, report, email, or update—ready for review.
- 02/
	### How does pricing work for Cowork?
	Copilot Cowork is available through usage-based billing, so it scales with the work you delegate.  
	  
	[Learn more](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?linkid=2366806)
- 03/
	### When should I use Cowork instead of Copilot Chat or an agent?
	Use Copilot Chat for quick answers and back-and-forth in the flow of your work. Use a specialized agent when you need a narrow, repeatable task automated. Use Cowork when the work spans multiple steps and apps—drafting, planning, coordinating, and producing finished artifacts you can review and ship.
- 04/
	### How does Cowork keep my data safe?
	Copilot Cowork inherits the enterprise-grade security, privacy, and compliance protections of Microsoft Copilot. Your data stays in your tenant and respects existing user and admin permissions. Cowork actions are auditable, and sensitive steps—like sending an email or updating a record—pause for your approval before they execute.
- 05/
	### How do I get started with Cowork?
	Cowork is available to Microsoft Copilot customers with usage-based billing. Admins can enable Cowork in the Microsoft 365 admin center, and end users can launch it from the Copilot app on web, desktop, or mobile.
