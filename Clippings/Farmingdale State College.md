#### Bring your friends and family to celebrate all things potatoes!

- Indulge in the "Alfresco Backyard Food Court" with dozens of potato dishes and all your festival favorites.
- Bounce around in our Bounce Park featuring slides, obstacle courses, sports, themed inflatables, games, and more!
- Check out the free Ninja Obstacle Course by No Limit Ninja.
- Enjoy live music and entertainment on the "Arrow Metal + eWaste Recycling Stage" filled with amazing local talent playing all the hits.
- Sip and enjoy the afternoon in the "ITP Beer Garden, run by Mulcahy's", for guests 21 and older, with corn hole and great views of the stage!
- Bring your tots to the "Tater Tot Lot, powered by Saf-T-Swim" for lawn games and activities!
- Stroll thru our Vendor Showcase filled with local companies, seasonal offerings, and more.
- Get fresh local specialties in "Farmer's market, presented by Hoshyla Farms"

#### POTATO MOUNTAIN!

See our potato mountain and Guess how many potatoes in Potato Mountain for your chance to win $500 from [Nassau Financial Federal Credit Union](https://www.nassaufinancial.org/).

SUNDAY AT 5PM - COME TO POTATO MOUNTAIN TO GET A FREE 5# BAG OF LONG ISLAND POTATOES (while supplies last).

The 1937 Riverhead Potato Festival featured a massive “Potato Mountain” made from more than 1,000 bushels of Long Island potatoes. At the end of the festival, visitors were invited to take home potatoes from the mountain. We’re bringing the tradition back! We’re giving away all the potatoes in our Potato Mountain on the last day of the festival!
