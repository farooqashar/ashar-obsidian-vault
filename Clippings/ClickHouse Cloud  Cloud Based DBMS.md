## Lower infra costs with ClickHouse Cloud

With cloud-native architecture, your infrastructure costs are lower than self-managed ClickHouse at scale.

#### Architecture designed for cost efficiency

Separation of storage and compute, pay-for-use pricing, and zero replica overhead mean you only pay for what you use.

#### Pay-for-use compute

CPU and memory scale up during load spikes and down during idle periods. No constant overprovisioning for peak traffic.

#### Less replica overhead

Object-backed parallel replicas in a shared-nothing architecture. Less storage duplication, less network overhead, better hardware utilization.

#### Compute-compute separation

Independent compute layers for read and write separation. Provision separate services for distinct workloads without interference.

#### Built-in integration framework

Managed, secure, and continuously updated integrations. Move data faster without the manual setup and maintenance of open source.

#### Better hardware utilization

Managed autoscaling, right-sized instances, and click-to-scale workflows. Same spend serves more queries than typical self-hosted deployments.
