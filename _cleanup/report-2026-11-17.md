### Verification

Then an independent content check that does not trust the script's own guards: each of the 26 filed notes was diffed byte-for-byte against its blob in `git HEAD`. **26/26 byte-exact** — original bytes plus exactly the intended tag lines inserted after `  - "clippings"`, nothing else touched, and the clipper's missing EOF newline preserved in every case.

One false alarm worth recording so it isn't re-investigated next week: `git diff --numstat -M` appeared to show every filed note as a 100% deletion. That is just git's view of an *unstaged* move — the old path reads as deleted, the new path as untracked, and `-M` rename detection does not apply to untracked files. Nothing was lost. The blob-level check above is the one that actually means something here; don't use `numstat` on this vault until obsidian-git has committed.
